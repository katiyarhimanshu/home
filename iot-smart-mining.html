IoT Applications in Smart Mining

Underground and open-cast mining environments present harsh physical hazards. Connected IoT sensor networks and resilient wireless telemetry are transforming worker safety and predictive maintenance.

The Need for Real-Time Mine Telemetry

Mining operations—especially deep underground tunnels and mineral-rich belts such as the Sonbhadra region—involve dynamic geological stresses, toxic gas accumulation, and heavy machinery operating in low-visibility zones. Traditional periodic manual inspections leave dangerous blind spots between checks.

Core Components of an Industrial Smart Mining Architecture

  • Environmental & Toxic Gas Sensing: Multi-gas sensor nodes continuously monitor methane (CH4), carbon monoxide (CO), hydrogen sulfide, ambient temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure, triggering automated ventilation fans and evacuation alarms before thresholds are breached.
  • Geotechnical & Structural Health Monitoring: MEMS accelerometers, gyroscopes, and roof-bolt strain gauges detect micro-seismic vibrations and strata movement early, helping prevent roof collapses.
  • Personnel & Asset Tracking: Ultra-Wideband (UWB) and BLE beacons integrated into miner helmets provide real-time localization during emergencies and prevent collisions with autonomous haulage vehicles.
  • Edge Telemetry & Cloud Dashboards: Embedded microcontrollers (such as ESP32-based industrial nodes) aggregate multi-sensor payloads and stream real-time telemetry to centralized platforms like ThingsBoard for live visualization and anomaly alerts.
Communication Challenges Underground: Curved tunnels, rock-wall attenuation, and machinery noise require hybrid wireless topologies—combining LoRaWAN, leaky feeder cables, and IEEE 802.15.4 mesh networks—to guarantee uninterrupted data delivery.

Impact on Safety and Sustainability

By shifting from reactive emergency response to predictive monitoring, IoT-enabled smart mines significantly reduce workplace accidents, optimize energy usage in ventilation systems, and minimize equipment downtime.
