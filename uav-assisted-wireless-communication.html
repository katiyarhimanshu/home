UAV-Assisted Wireless Communication

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) are evolving from aerial cameras into agile flying base stations—delivering on-demand connectivity where terrestrial towers cannot reach.

Why Take Base Stations Airborne?

Terrestrial cellular towers are fixed infrastructure: expensive to deploy in rugged terrain and vulnerable during natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, or landslides. UAV-mounted transceivers overcome these limitations by offering rapid, three-dimensional deployment and high-probability Line-of-Sight (LoS) air-to-ground links.

Primary Use Cases in 5G and 6G

  • Emergency & Disaster Recovery Networks: Within minutes of terrestrial network failure, tethered or autonomous drone swarms can restore emergency voice, SMS, and situational telemetry for first responders.
  • Hotspot Capacity Offloading: During temporary mass gatherings, festivals, or sporting events, aerial small cells relieve congestion on ground macrocells without permanent capital expenditure.
  • IoT Data Harvesting in Remote Areas: UAVs act as mobile data mules, flying over agricultural fields, forest reserves, or remote sensor grids to collect low-power sensor transmissions efficiently.
  • Aerial IRS & Relays: Carrying lightweight Intelligent Reflecting Surfaces or mmWave relays enables drones to bridge blockages in dense urban canyons.
Key Engineering Trade-off: Jointly optimizing 3D flight trajectory, transmit power, and propulsion energy consumption is essential, as onboard battery capacity remains the primary constraint on flight endurance.

Future Direction

Integrating AI-driven autonomous path planning with non-terrestrial satellite networks (NTN) will make UAVs a vital middle layer in seamless space-air-ground integrated networks (SAGIN).
